critique: backs a judgement of the film with something specific in it (an actor, character, director, scene, plot point, the ending, the style, another named film, or a filming or technical choice) and says what that thing is like or why, not just that it was good or bad
hot_take: states a verdict on the film without pointing to anything specific in it
experience: mainly reports a reaction the film caused in the writer or the people they watched with (crying, falling asleep, walking out) rather than judging the film, and points to nothing specific in it
